## Special Term Highlights

### Nuanced Translations

Some terms have been carefully translated to capture Korean cultural nuances:

**Three Types of Jealousy:**

- 질투 = jealous of what you have (fear of losing it)
- 시기 = jealous of what you don't have (wanting what others have)
- 부러움 = admiring jealousy (positive envy without bitterness)

**Three Ways to Say "Passed Away":**

- 별세 = passed away (with respect, for honored people)
- 객사 = passed away (neutral, died away from home)
- 하늘 나라에 돌아간다 = returned to heaven (Christian euphemism)

**Visual Impairment Distinctions:**

- 실눈 = squinted (partially closing eyes)
- 소경 = blind (complete blindness)
